📖 Goal Calculations Guide

We calculate your targets based on the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation to identify your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), and scale it using your Activity Factor to identify your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE):

  • Weight Loss: TDEE - 500 kcal. Promotes safe fat loss at ~0.5kg per week.
  • Weight Gain: TDEE + 500 kcal. Supports weight increases.
  • Muscle Gain: TDEE + 300 kcal. Surplus optimized for lean skeletal mass additions.
  • Maintenance: Balanced TDEE matching your daily caloric output.
